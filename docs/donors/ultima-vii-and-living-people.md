# Living people pass

The first generalized schedule implementation follows the useful Ultima VII
principle that schedules are intentions, not teleports.  A scheduled target is
resolved through the route graph; an actor enters transit, spends route time,
and only then arrives.  Schedule priority supports work over ordinary leisure,
and an interruption stores a temporary intention with an expiry rather than
destroying the underlying schedule.

The micro-world fixture combines that mechanism with ordinary household places,
workplaces, a tavern, a shop, a workshop, a warehouse, a dock, and a vessel.
It is intentionally a test fixture, not a historical reconstruction.
